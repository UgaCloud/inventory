What the platform covers

The whole day-to-day of running a trading business, from the goods arriving in the store to the invoice leaving the counter.

Stock & inventory

Live quantities per branch, reorder levels that warn you before you run out, batch tracking, adjustments and transfers between stores.

Sales & purchasing

Record a sale in seconds, raise purchase orders against a supplier and keep every receipt, invoice and return in one ledger.

Finance

Expenses, bank accounts, cash flow and customer credit — with figures that match the stock movement behind them.

Staff & permissions

Roles decide what each person can open. Nobody sees a menu item they are not allowed to use, and every change is written down.

Multi-branch

Branches, stores and departments share one catalogue while keeping their own stock, staff and cost picture.

Audit trail

Every record change, sign-in and export is recorded with who did it, when, and what changed. Entries can never be edited away.

How access works

Accounts are created by your platform administrator, so your data never ends up in the wrong business.

Ask your administrator

Your platform administrator registers the business and creates your first account.

You get a role

Your administrator assigns you a role, which decides the modules you can open.

You sign in

Sign in with your own username. Your branch, business and permissions are applied for you.

Need an account? Accounts are not self-service — contact the platform administrator for your business and they will create one for you and set your role.
